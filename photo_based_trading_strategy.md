# Strategy Based on the Shared Chart Photo

## What the chart appears to show
- **Instrument/timeframe:** ETH on an intraday chart (looks like around 15m).
- **Marked zones:**
  - **Orange bands** appear to be supply/resistance zones.
  - **Green bands** appear to be demand/support and target zones.
- **Pattern context:** strong bullish impulse, then range/chop, then rejection from upper zone and reaction near support.

## Core idea (zone reaction strategy)
Use a simple **support/resistance zone reaction plan**:
1. **Sell near orange supply** only after bearish confirmation.
2. **Buy near green demand** only after bullish confirmation.
3. Take first profits at the next opposing zone.
4. Trail remainder only if momentum continues.

## Entry rules
### A) Short setup (at orange zone)
- Price touches or wicks into an orange zone.
- Wait for confirmation on entry timeframe (e.g., 5m/15m):
  - bearish engulfing candle, or
  - lower high + breakdown of minor support.
- Enter short only after confirmation close.

### B) Long setup (at green zone)
- Price drops into a green zone.
- Wait for confirmation:
  - bullish engulfing/strong rejection wick, or
  - higher low + break of minor swing high.
- Enter long after confirmation close.

## Stop-loss and targets
- **Stop-loss:** just outside the zone edge + a small buffer.
  - For shorts: above orange zone high.
  - For longs: below green zone low.
- **TP1:** nearest opposing zone (high-probability partial exit).
- **TP2:** next zone beyond TP1 if structure/momentum supports continuation.
- Move stop to breakeven after TP1.

## Risk management (most important)
- Risk only **0.5%–1% per trade**.
- Avoid entries in the middle of ranges (low edge).
- Maximum 2 failed attempts per session on same setup.
- Skip trades during major high-impact news.

## Example execution from this chart style
- If price revisits an **upper orange zone** and rejects with bearish confirmation, take a short targeting the nearest **green level**.
- If price sweeps a **green demand zone** and reclaims it, take a long targeting the nearest **orange level**.

## Quick checklist before every trade
- Is price at a marked zone (not mid-range)?
- Did confirmation candle/structure appear?
- Is R:R at least **1:2** to TP1/TP2?
- Is SL placed outside zone with buffer?
- Is position size aligned with 0.5%–1% risk?
